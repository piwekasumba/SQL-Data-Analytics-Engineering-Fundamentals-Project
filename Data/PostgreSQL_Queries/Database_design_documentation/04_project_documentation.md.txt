## Project Purpose

This project was built to strengthen my practical SQL skills by working through realistic business reporting scenarios using PostgreSQL.

The focus is on designing relational databases, writing structured SQL queries, preparing reporting-ready data, and analysing business information to support operational reporting and data-driven decision-making.

The project reflects the foundational SQL, reporting, and analytical skills expected of an aspiring entry-level Reporting Analyst.
